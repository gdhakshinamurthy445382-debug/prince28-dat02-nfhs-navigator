
# NFHS Navigator — 10 Test Questions

These questions will be used to test the accuracy of NFHS Navigator.

1. What is the percentage of children under five years who are stunted in a selected Tamil Nadu district according to NFHS-5?

2. What is the percentage of children under five years who are wasted in a selected district according to NFHS-5?

3. What is the percentage of children under five years who are underweight in a selected district according to NFHS-5?

4. What is the prevalence of anaemia among children aged 6–59 months in a selected district according to NFHS-5?

5. What is the prevalence of anaemia among women aged 15–49 years in a selected district according to NFHS-5?

6. What is the prevalence of anaemia among pregnant women aged 15–49 years in a selected district according to NFHS-5?

7. How does the prevalence of anaemia among women differ between two selected Tamil Nadu districts?

8. What is the difference in child stunting between urban and rural areas in a selected district?

9. How has the prevalence of anaemia among women changed between NFHS-4 and NFHS-5 in a selected district, measured in percentage points?

10. Which Tamil Nadu districts have the highest and lowest prevalence of anaemia among children aged 6–59 months according to NFHS-5?

## Testing Rule

Every answer must include the NFHS round, district, indicator, area, source table and page reference. The system must not guess missing values.
